Rules & Policies

A few house rules that apply across both the Boys and Girls wings.

Entry / Exit Timing

Fixed gate-closing times apply each wing; late entry needs prior notice to the warden.

Visitor Policy

Guests are welcome in the common area during set daytime hours, logged at the front desk. No overnight stays.

Smoking & Alcohol

Strictly not allowed anywhere on the premises.

Cleanliness

Residents are expected to keep personal spaces tidy; housekeeping covers common areas daily.

Noise Restrictions

Quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM out of respect for other residents.

Payment Policy

Rent is due by the 5th of every month; a one-month deposit is collected at move-in and refunded on move-out.
