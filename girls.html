Your Safety Is Our Priority

We built Prerana's Girls PG around one idea: parents should feel as at ease as their daughters do. Every layer below runs continuously, not just on request.

  • 24/7 CCTV monitoring at every entrance and common area
  • Secure biometric access — only residents and staff can enter
  • Visitor management — every guest is logged and approved
  • Round-the-clock emergency contact number for residents
  • Well-maintained, well-lit premises inside and out

Rules & Policies

A few house rules that keep the PG comfortable and secure for everyone.

Entry / Exit Timing

Gates close at 9:30 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on weekends. Late entry needs prior notice to the warden.

Visitor Policy

Guests are welcome in the ground-floor common area between 10 AM and 6 PM, logged at the front desk. No visitors in rooms.

Smoking & Alcohol

Strictly not allowed anywhere on the premises.

Cleanliness

Residents are expected to keep personal spaces tidy; housekeeping covers common areas daily.

Noise Restrictions

Quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM out of respect for other residents.

Payment Policy

Rent is due by the 5th of every month; a one-month deposit is collected at move-in and refunded on move-out.
