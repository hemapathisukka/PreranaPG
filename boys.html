Rules & Policies

A few house rules that keep the PG comfortable for everyone.

Entry / Exit Timing

Gates close at 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:30 PM on weekends. Late entry needs prior notice to the warden.

Visitor Policy

Guests are welcome in the common area between 10 AM and 7 PM with prior intimation. No overnight stays.

Smoking & Alcohol

Strictly not allowed anywhere on the premises, including balconies and the terrace.

Cleanliness

Residents are expected to keep personal spaces tidy; housekeeping covers common areas daily.

Noise Restrictions

Quiet hours from 10 PM to 7 AM out of respect for other residents.

Payment Policy

Rent is due by the 5th of every month; a one-month deposit is collected at move-in and refunded on move-out.
